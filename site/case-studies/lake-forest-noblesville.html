At Commercial Recreation Group, we believe that play time is essential to the well being of all youngsters, regardless of physical limitations. This is why we offer a wide variety of ADA compliant play structures, ramps, and surfacing. Our commercial division recently had the opportunity to construct a fully ADA compliant playground in Noblesville, IN. This new playground structure is wheelchair accessible and features wood mulch and timbers for an aesthetically pleasing, natural look. We completed the sidewalks for this project as well.

If you’re interested in learning more about ADA compliance, and would like to make sure your commercial playground is up to current standards, give us a call at (317) 776-7830. We’d love to help you ensure quality play time for everyone.

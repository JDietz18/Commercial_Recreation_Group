Playground equipment

Modular and custom play structures for early childhood (2–5) and school-age (5–12) users: slides, climbers, rope nets, swings and freestanding play events, including inclusive and accessible options.

  • Early childhood and school-age structures
  • Inclusive and accessible play
  • Swings, spinners and freestanding events
  • Rope and net climbers

Themed & custom play

From farm barns to timber towers, themed structures give a park or campus a landmark of its own. We work with you on the concept, colors and play value.

  • Theme and color development
  • Custom towers and roof forms
  • Natural timber looks

Shade structures

Shade over play areas, seating and splash pads extends the hours a site can be used and protects kids from the sun. Options range from single-post umbrellas to large hip and sail canopies.

  • Shade sails and hip canopies
  • Shelters and pavilions
  • Shade over existing equipment

  • Professional installation
